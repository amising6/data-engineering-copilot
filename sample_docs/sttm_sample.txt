Business Rules:

Latest Customer Record:

PARTITION BY DIM_CUSTOMER_KEY
ORDER BY CUSTOMER_EFF_DT DESC
)=1

Latest Account Record:

PARTITION BY DIM_ACCOUNT_KEY
ORDER BY ACCOUNT_EFF_DT DESC
)=1
